KWrap style guides / Two-tone kitchens

Two-tone kitchens

Two finishes in one kitchen make it look designed rather than fitted. The kitchen above combines white with a dark wood grain. See kitchen wrapping.

Popular combinations

  • Dark base units, light wall units.
  • A contrast island in a single-colour kitchen.
  • A solid colour paired with a wood grain.

Keep it simple

Two finishes plus the worktop is usually enough. A third colour tends to make a kitchen look busy.

Plinths and panels

Decide whether plinths and end panels follow the base colour or the wall colour; following the base units usually looks neater.

Questions

Which colour goes on top?

Usually the lighter one, which keeps the room feeling open.
